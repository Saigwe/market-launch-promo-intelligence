# Two-minute walkthrough

1. **Executive brief.** Corvane's value share is up 3.3 points across markets. Germany is up 8.9: the headline story.
2. **Market & share, Germany.** The reported line jumps in one week in April. The comparable line does not. Elektra
   Markt joined the panel that week, and Corvane sells well there.
3. **The bridge.** Of 8.9 points, 6.8 are panel coverage; 2.1 survive on retailers covered in both years: real, and in
   line with other markets, driven by the Arc Buds launch.
4. **Weekly against monthly.** The independent monthly series tracks the weekly panel within 2% except one cell, which is
   flagged to the provider.
5. **Price gaps.** UK earbuds are the one place share fell: Askel cut its earbuds by 15% in June and the gap widened from
   about 7% to 26%.
6. **Launches.** Arc Buds outsold Pulse Buds' first 12 weeks everywhere but Japan, where two retailers listed it late.
7. **Promotions.** Medium-depth promotions earned margin; deep ones did not once the dip after is counted. The intervals
   are wide. Black Friday SKU promotions inside a store-wide event look twice as good as they were unless the overlap
   is handled. The planned calendar shows its likely range.
