# Executive brief: what grew, and how sure are we

*Demo results on synthetic data. Figures from [results.md](results.md) and [promo_effects.md](promo_effects.md).*

**Headline.** Corvane's value share rose about 3 points across five markets in the latest 13 weeks against a year
earlier. Germany's reported gain of 8.9 points is mostly a change in what the panel measures.

**What the evidence supports**

- **Germany:** of the 8.9-point gain, 2.1 points survive on retailers covered in both years. The rest is Elektra
  Markt entering the panel in April 2026. The real gain is in line with France and the US.
- **UK earbuds** are the one place Corvane lost share (-0.8 points against +8 points in other markets). Askel's 15% cut
  on its flagship earbuds in June widened the price gap from about 7% to 26%.
- **Arc Buds** outsold the previous launch in four markets. In Japan it reached 80% of Pulse Buds' first 12 weeks,
  because two of four retailers listed it ten weeks late.
- **Promotions:** medium-depth promotions (15-24%) earned about $270k of incremental margin; deep and shallow ones lost
  a little each, once the dip after is counted. Intervals are wide, and three Black Friday promotions looked about
  1.7 times better than they were until the store-wide event they ran inside was separated out.

**Recommendations.** Report Germany on the comparable panel until a year has passed. Respond to Askel in the UK only.
Secure listings before the next launch in Japan. Keep promotions in the 15-24% band and plan next Black Friday on
attributed, not naive, lift.

**Uncertainty.** Lifts are estimates from observational data; the 95% intervals contain the true lift in about 88% of
cases here, so treat them as a lower bound. The overlap split is an assumption.
